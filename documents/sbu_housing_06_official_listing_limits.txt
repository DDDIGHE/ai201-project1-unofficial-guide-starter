Title: SBU off-campus listing access and limits
Source: https://www.reddit.com/r/SBU/comments/13819jj

A parent asked whether someone could help view SBU's off-campus listing site because it was accessible only to enrolled students or faculty and staff.

One commenter said the site is where SBU students and faculty post sublets or rentals. Another commenter said there were only a small number of listings visible, many with year-long leases, and mentioned a $1600 per month apartment and a house far away.

The thread also included a student offering an off-campus room for summer at $800 plus utilities with kitchen, bathroom, and laundry access.
