Title: Cheap close housing without a car
Source: https://www.reddit.com/r/SBU/comments/1bq4xi3

A student asked how to find cheap housing close to Stony Brook without a car and said they preferred to live with women.

One reply said the Stony Brook Housing Facebook group is the student's friend. Another commenter mentioned a room in a house with four other women for $950 per month without utilities, furnished, with a bathroom, and a five-minute walk from Lot 40.
