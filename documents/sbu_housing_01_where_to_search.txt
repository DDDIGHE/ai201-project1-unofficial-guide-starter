Title: Where to search off-campus housing
Source: https://www.reddit.com/r/SBU/comments/1t4dx0z/off_campus_housing_for_sbu/

An incoming international undergrad asked where to search for off-campus housing near Stony Brook because on-campus housing felt too expensive.

The main reply warned that finding anything cheaper than the dorms can be difficult unless the student joins a group of four to eight people and rents a whole house together. The reply also said Long Island is expensive and that a one-bedroom apartment near SBU is usually more than $2000 per month.

Another reply suggested searching the SBU subreddit for prior offers and making a new post if direct messages do not come in.
