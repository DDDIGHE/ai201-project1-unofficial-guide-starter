Title: Need off-campus housing after missing campus housing
Source: https://www.reddit.com/r/SBU/comments/1dezhk7

A student said they could not get on-campus housing for fall and asked if anyone needed an off-campus roommate.

One reply said the Stony Brook Housing Facebook group has local landlords, realtors, and students posting rooms in houses. The reply said that is where they found their own place, but also warned that the group is full of scammers, so students should be careful.
