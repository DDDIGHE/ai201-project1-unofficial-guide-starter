Title: First-time international students looking for a house
Source: https://www.reddit.com/r/SBU/comments/1b7oyij

A group of four to six international students asked whether the SBU off-campus housing page, Zillow, and ForRentUniversity were safe places to search. Their criteria were a maximum of $6000 total, a safe area, and a mostly furnished place.

One reply recommended Facebook groups for Stony Brook off-campus housing and said to watch for people asking for money before a house is seen or asking for money to secure the house.

The same reply said students without cars should stay in the Stony Brook area, especially around Stony Brook Road. It also listed Zillow, Realtor.com, and Trulia as search sites.

Another reply said private Facebook groups have fewer scammers and that the SBU off-campus site may be more secure but prices can be higher.
