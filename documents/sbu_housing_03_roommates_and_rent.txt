Title: Housing quality and roommate costs
Source: https://www.reddit.com/r/SBU/comments/1rjtnja/off_campus_housing/

A student asked how bad housing around SBU is with roommates after becoming unhappy with a current place.

One reply said Stony Brook, Setauket, Port Jefferson, and nearby areas are generally nice, and that the bigger issue is high rent. The reply estimated that a decent four- or five-bedroom house costs about $4500 to $6500 per month in total.

A longer reply warned that landlords can be shady, appearances can be deceptive, and students should document the move-in condition from the first day to protect the security deposit. The same reply warned that old houses can have very high gas heating bills and that cheap houses may cost more over time if maintenance is poor.
