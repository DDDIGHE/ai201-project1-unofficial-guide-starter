Title: Housing post near the SBU bus
Source: https://www.reddit.com/r/SBU/comments/1dc91y1

A student posted that many people ask where to find housing and warned that there are scams, so students should check the place in person.

The post said Facebook groups and Reddit are common places where people advertise. The specific offer was two bedrooms for two people at $1950 total, no car needed, and within walking distance of the Stony Brook bus.

A reply reacted to someone forfeiting a security deposit.
