Title: Looking for an $850 to $900 room
Source: https://www.reddit.com/r/SBU/comments/1jkuzt4

A student asked where to look for off-campus housing with a budget around $850 to $900.

The reply said that at that price the student would probably need a bedroom in a shared house. It said those rooms are often not on Zillow or Apartments.com and may need to be found through SBU housing Facebook groups, Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist, bus stop posters around campus, or word of mouth.

The reply warned that students should never pay an application fee or deposit before seeing the place in person.
